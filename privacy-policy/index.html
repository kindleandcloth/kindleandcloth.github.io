Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit kindleandcloth.github.io ("the site").

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and the site has no contact forms, comment sections, or newsletter signups. We do use Google Analytics, and we load Pinterest's official Save button script, both described below. If advertising is added to the site, Google and its partners may also use cookies as described in the advertising section.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect basic technical information, such as your IP address, for security and to deliver the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Fonts

Our fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your information to a third-party font service.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-VFH3PJBQ5L) to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are read, roughly where visitors are, and what kind of browser or device they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect identifiers such as IP addresses. This information is processed by Google. You can read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Search

Site search runs in your browser. It uses a list of our guides that we host on this site, and it does not send your search to a third-party search company. If you submit a search, the words may appear in the page address and in our host's ordinary server logs.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads on this site through Google AdSense. When those ads are present, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

To learn more about how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Until AdSense ads are approved and placed on the site, Google advertising cookies from AdSense are not used here. Other cookies described in this policy, including Google Analytics and Pinterest, may still be used.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

When ads are shown, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland will see a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before advertising cookies are used, as required by Google's EU User Consent Policy. That message will let you accept or decline the use of cookies and personal data for ads personalization.

Affiliate links and third-party sites

Some links go to other websites, including retailers such as Amazon and brands in the Awin network. When you click an affiliate link, that site and the affiliate network may use cookies or similar technologies to record that you came from Kindle & Cloth, so a commission can be credited if you make a purchase. Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. See our affiliate disclosure for more about those links.

Pinterest

We load Pinterest's official Save button script (from assets.pinterest.com), asynchronously, so you can save images and guides from this site to a Pinterest board. If you use a Save button, or if you interact with us on Pinterest, Pinterest's privacy policy applies to that activity.

Children

This site is intended for adults planning weddings and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

For privacy questions, message us on Pinterest at @kindleandcloth.
