A short

How editing works

  1. 1

    Download the file

    After you buy, open Etsy in a browser — not the app — and download the PDF from your purchases page.

  2. 2

    Open it in Canva

    The PDF has a link that makes your own copy. A free Canva account is enough; you do not need Canva Pro.

  3. 3

    Add your wording

    Type your names, dates, drinks, table numbers, or a photo-album link. Keep the art as it is, or move a text box if you need to.

  4. 4

    Print it

    Download a print-quality PDF and print at home, or take it to a print shop or an online printer. Nothing is mailed.

A few

Questions we hear

How do Canva templates work?

You get a file, not a printed sign. The PDF links to a Canva copy you can edit in a browser. Change the text, download your finished file, and print it yourself.

Do I need Canva Pro?

No. These templates are set up for a free Canva account.

Is anything shipped?

No. This is an instant digital download. You'll find the file in your Etsy purchases, in a browser, once the order is confirmed.

Where should I print?

At home for a smaller table sign or a 5-by-7 card, or at a print shop or online printer for a large easel size. If you're printing on a home printer, start with the size that fits the piece — often the 8-by-10 or the 5-by-7.
