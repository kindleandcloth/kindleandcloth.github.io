About Kindle & Cloth

Kindle & Cloth is a wedding idea blog for couples who'd rather say "I do" under the trees than in a ballroom. We focus on five things: backyard weddings, rustic invitations, outdoor decor, small weddings, and eco-friendly weddings.

To browse the guides by what you need, start with the hubs: wedding food and recipes, holiday and winter weddings, gift guides, backyard planning, and decor and DIY. Editable signs and stationery from our shop live on the wedding templates page.

What you'll find here

  • Practical guides. Checklists, layouts, timelines, and the questions to ask vendors, written to be useful on a real planning weekend.
  • Cozy, outdoor style. String lights, long tables, textured paper, greenery, and celebrations that feel personal.
  • Budget-aware ideas. What to rent, what to borrow, and where spending makes the biggest difference.
  • Lower-waste choices. Ways to cut paper, reuse decor, and pick materials with less impact.

How we choose what to recommend

We point to products and services that fit the kind of weddings we write about, and we describe them plainly. We don't publish made-up reviews, ratings, or prices. Prices and policies change, so always check the retailer's own page before you buy.
